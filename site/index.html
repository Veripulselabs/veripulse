Questions & Answers

            
Frequently Asked Questions.

            
Everything you need to know about our detection architecture, privacy, and support.

        


        

            
            

                
                    How fast is VeriPulse in live production?
                    +
                
                

                    Under 40 milliseconds average turnaround. We execute in-memory syntax checks, disposable domain blocklist lookups, and asynchronous non-blocking UDP DNS MX queries so user signups stay lightning-fast without lagging user experience.
                

            


            
            

                
                    How does disposable & temporary email detection work?
                    +
                
                

                    We maintain an actively updated catalog of over 50,000+ known disposable domains (including Mailinator, GuerillaMail, TempMail, and 10MinuteMail) coupled with domain entropy heuristics to prevent fraudulent bot signups and free trial abuse.
                

            


            
            

                
                    Is VeriPulse GDPR and CCPA compliant?
                    +
                
                

                    Yes. VeriPulse operates on strict zero-retention data privacy. We never store, log, or share the email addresses evaluated through our API. Verification checks are evaluated entirely in volatile memory and instantly discarded.
                

            


            
            

                
                    Can I integrate VeriPulse with my tech stack?
                    +
                
                

                    Yes. VeriPulse is a standard HTTP REST API that returns lightweight JSON payloads. You can plug it into Next.js, Python, Ruby, PHP, Node.js, Go, or client-side form triggers in under 5 minutes. Ready-to-copy code snippets are available in our Developers section.
