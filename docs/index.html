Engine Architecture

            
Precision down to the millisecond.

        


        
        

            
            

                

                    01 / SHIELD
                    
50,000+ Disposable List

                    

                        Instant identification of throwaway addresses from Mailinator, GuerrillaMail, 10MinuteMail, and over 50,000 active disposable domains.
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                    02 / DNS
                    
Live MX Record Discovery

                    

                        Asynchronous UDP DNS workers confirm destination mail exchangers exist and accept traffic without stalling your user onboarding flow.
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                    03 / PARSER
                    
RFC 5322 & Typo Healing

                    

                        Strict regex syntax validation combined with algorithmic domain distance matching to catch accidental keystrokes before submission.
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                    04 / RISK
                    
Weighted Scoring Engine

                    

                        Composite fraud calculation combining syntax, disposable tags, free provider markers, and MX presence into an unambiguous 0–100 score.
